Currently, I am a **master’s student in AI Transformation (AX) at KAIST**, conducting research at the **Visual Media Lab** under the advisement of **Professor Junyong Noh**. Previously, I worked as a research intern in the same lab. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from **Konkuk University**, where I conducted undergraduate research on real-time rendering under Professor Hyungseok Kim. I also gained research experience in machine learning under Professor Haewon Byun at Sungshin Women’s University.

Research Interests
======
My research interests lie at the intersection of **Computer Graphics and Deep Learning**, with a focus on **3D facial reconstruction, generation, and modeling**, **facial retargeting and animation transfer**, and **neural rendering**. I am interested in developing realistic digital humans through expressive representations and high-quality rendering. Currently, I am working on **head and full-body reconstruction and avatar animation**.
